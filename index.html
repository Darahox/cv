About

Profile

A versatile scientist and educator whose background spans animal communication, neuroethology, physiology, ecology, entomology, and nursing science. Brings PhD-level research in neuroethology, hands-on clinical nursing experience across ICU, cardiac, and rehabilitation settings, and a demonstrated record of innovative course design. Deeply invested in active learning, undergraduate research mentorship, and the practical integration of AI tools into evidence-based pedagogy.
Doctoral candidate in neuroethology at the University of Missouri, investigating the neural circuits underlying acoustic communication in Neoconocephalus katydids. Research integrates neuroanatomy, electrophysiology, and behavioral ecology. Experienced in mentoring undergraduate researchers and presenting findings at national venues.
Registered Nurse (Missouri licensed) with hands-on experience in ICU, cardiac catheterization and electrophysiology, and inpatient rehabilitation. Skilled in total patient care, interdisciplinary collaboration, individualized care planning, and patient/caregiver education. Brings a scientist's rigor to evidence-based clinical practice.
Versatile science educator with a demonstrated record of course development, active learning design, and exceptional teaching effectiveness. Created a 200-student human physiology laboratory from scratch. Teaching effectiveness scores consistently 76th–89th percentile among all Division of Biological Sciences graduate TAs.
Co-creator of Imagination is the Key, a publicly searchable database of 200+ real AI-in-teaching experiments. Workshop leader at national faculty development venues. Designs and demonstrates live AI classroom tools including agentic browser systems, discipline-specific course chatbots, and interactive learning modules.
